Our Tiling Process

1. Surface Preparation: Ensuring a clean, level base for proper installation.

2. Layout Planning: Careful measurement for perfect alignment and spacing.

3. Tile Installation: Strong adhesive application and precise placement.

4. Grouting: Filling joints neatly for a clean and sealed finish.

5. Final Cleaning: Polishing the surface for a professional look.
